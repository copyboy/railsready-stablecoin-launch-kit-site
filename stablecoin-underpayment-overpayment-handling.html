Stablecoin payment launch playbook

Stablecoin underpayment and overpayment handling

Stablecoin payments often look simple until the amount does not match. Customers can send too little, too much, duplicate a transfer, pay after quote expiry, or include dust amounts that break exact-match reconciliation. The product, finance, provider, and support teams need one shared state model.

Amount mismatch states

Do not reduce every mismatch to failed or paid. A useful state model separates underpaid, overpaid, duplicate, late, dust, pending review, manually credited, refunded, and rejected outcomes. Each state needs an owner and a ledger rule.

Rules to document

  • Minimum accepted amount and tolerance threshold.
  • Whether the customer can top up the difference after an underpayment.
  • Whether overpayments are refunded, credited, or held for review.
  • How quote expiry affects detected transfers.
  • How duplicate tx hashes or reused addresses are handled.

Finance impact

An overpayment is not free revenue, and an underpayment is not always an automatic rejection. Finance needs to know whether the provider settles the detected amount, the expected amount, or a net converted amount after fees and FX spread.

Support scripts

  1. Tell the customer what was expected and what was detected.
  2. Explain whether additional payment, refund, or review is available.
  3. Avoid promising instant credit while provider review is open.
  4. Give a ticket reference and next review window.

Launch review test

Before launch, demonstrate one underpayment, one overpayment, one duplicate transfer, and one late payment through the provider webhook, internal ledger, and support flow.
