Stablecoin payment launch playbook

USDC and USDT checkout provider comparison questions

Provider comparison pages often focus on fees and brand names. A real checkout pilot needs harder questions: supported chains, address assignment, KYB/KYC, KYT, refunds, webhook reliability, settlement references, and who is allowed to say what to customers.

Compare capabilities, not slogans

The best provider for one merchant may be a poor fit for another. A game company top-up flow, SaaS marketplace checkout, cross-border settlement flow, and payout flow can each need different custody, risk, reporting, and refund behavior.

Comparison categories

  • Supported jurisdictions, legal entity, onboarding requirements, and restricted business types.
  • Supported tokens, chains, address assignment, QR format, and quote expiry.
  • Webhook lifecycle, idempotency guidance, dashboard exports, and reporting API.
  • Wrong-chain, wrong-token, underpay, overpay, duplicate, refund, and risk rejection handling.
  • Settlement currency, payout timing, payout references, fees, and reconciliation exports.

Questions for vendor calls

  1. Who controls private keys and deposit addresses?
  2. Can we test every exception state before launch?
  3. How do provider order IDs map to tx hashes and payout references?
  4. What exact customer wording do you approve or require?
  5. What happens if a customer pays after quote expiry?

Scoring providers

Use a weighted matrix. Give higher weight to evidence quality and exception handling than to headline fees. A cheaper provider that cannot explain reconciliation can become expensive during the first incident.
