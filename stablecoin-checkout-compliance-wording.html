Stablecoin checkout compliance wording checklist

Customer-facing copy can accidentally create regulated-service claims. A checkout page that says the wrong thing about custody, exchange, instant settlement, refunds, or licensing can create more risk than the integration itself.

Claims to avoid

  • "We custody your crypto" unless the exact licensed entity and service boundary are approved.
  • "We exchange your crypto for fiat" if a provider performs conversion.
  • "Instant settlement" when confirmations, provider review, banking rails, or risk checks can delay posting.
  • "No risk" or "guaranteed payment" because wrong-chain transfers, unsupported tokens, and risk rejections still happen.
  • "Licensed" or "regulated" unless counsel confirms the exact entity, jurisdiction, and wording.

Safer wording patterns

Use precise operational language. Say that stablecoin payments are processed by a payment provider. Show the token and network near the address. Explain that payments may require network confirmations and provider review. Warn that sending the wrong token or wrong network may delay or prevent settlement.

Checkout UI requirements

  1. Show token, chain, amount, expiry, and address in the same visible block.
  2. Make the copy button copy only the address, not extra prose.
  3. Confirm QR code and text address match.
  4. Link to refund and support policy near the payment instructions.
  5. Use a neutral status model: waiting, detected, reviewing, settled, failed, refunded.

Support scripts matter

The first customer support ticket often arrives when a user sends funds on the wrong network or sees a transaction confirmed but the order still pending. Support needs approved language for "payment detected," "under review," "wrong network," "refund requested," and "provider rejected." Do not improvise these messages during a live incident.

Launch review

Before launching, read every checkout, help-center, onboarding, and sales sentence out loud and ask whether it implies your company provides custody, exchange, issuance, yield, legal certainty, or guaranteed settlement. If the answer is maybe, rewrite it before production.
