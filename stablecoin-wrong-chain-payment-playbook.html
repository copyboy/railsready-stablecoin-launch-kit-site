Stablecoin wrong-chain payment playbook

Wrong-chain payments are one of the fastest ways for a stablecoin pilot to become a support incident. A customer may send the right token on an unsupported network, send the wrong token to a supported address, or reuse an old address after expiry. The playbook needs to exist before the first live customer funds move.

Define the exception before launch

A wrong-chain transfer should not be handled as a vague manual escalation. Define whether the provider can detect the transfer, whether recovery is technically possible, who pays recovery costs, who approves remediation, and what wording support can use with the customer.

Minimum data to capture

  • Customer order or payment intent ID.
  • Displayed token, displayed chain, amount, address, and expiry timestamp.
  • Actual token, actual chain, transaction hash, source address, and confirmation status.
  • Provider detection status, recovery status, risk status, and support ticket ID.
  • Final outcome: credited, refunded, rejected, unrecoverable, or manually remediated.

Customer-facing wording

Use precise language. Avoid promising recovery until the provider confirms it. Tell the customer that sending funds on an unsupported network may delay or prevent settlement. Keep the support response calm, factual, and tied to provider review rather than chain speculation.

Launch review test

  1. Show a test payment sent on the supported token and chain.
  2. Show the support path for a wrong-chain transfer.
  3. Show who decides whether to credit the customer before recovery completes.
  4. Show how the case is excluded from normal daily reconciliation until resolved.

Decision rule

If the team cannot explain wrong-chain handling without improvising, the flow should stay in sandbox. RailsReady treats wrong-chain handling as a required exception gate, not a rare edge case.
