Stablecoin payment support runbook

Support is where stablecoin pilots become real. Customers do not care that the transfer is visible onchain if the product still says pending. A support runbook keeps the first incident from turning into improvised policy.

Ticket categories

  • Payment not detected.
  • Detected onchain but pending provider review.
  • Wrong token or wrong chain.
  • Underpayment, overpayment, duplicate, or late payment.
  • Refund requested or remediation needed.
  • Settlement or balance credit delayed.

What support can ask for

Support can ask for public transaction hashes, order IDs, and screenshots of non-sensitive status pages when approved. Support should not ask for private keys, seed phrases, passwords, wallet signatures, browser cookies, customer PII in public channels, or confidential bank records.

Approved response pattern

  1. Acknowledge the issue and collect the safe identifier.
  2. State what has been detected and what is still under review.
  3. Give the next review window or escalation owner.
  4. Avoid promising recovery, settlement, refund, or credit before the provider status supports it.

Internal escalation

Each ticket category should map to one owner: provider operations, finance, engineering, compliance, or product. A support agent should never need to decide custody, refund, or regulated-service wording alone.

Readiness test

Before launch, run mock tickets for the top five exceptions and confirm support can answer using approved copy without seeing secrets or production customer data.
