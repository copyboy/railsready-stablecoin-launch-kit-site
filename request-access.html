Stablecoin payment launch playbook

Request RailsReady Launch Kit access

The live payment link is not enabled yet, but teams can already request access, describe a public-safe pilot shape, and get routed to the right product tier. Do not include confidential contracts, customer data, private keys, wallet secrets, bank records, or production transaction data.

What to share publicly

Use the GitHub request form for non-sensitive buying signals only: company type, flow type, provider shortlist, target token and chain, rough pilot timeline, and main launch concern. A GitHub issue is public, so keep it generic.

Product tiers

  • Launch Kit, $149: self-serve templates for one team evaluating one pilot.
  • Operator Pack, $499: template pack plus filled sample and decision memo outline.
  • Blueprint License, $1,499: internal-use license for a payment team that needs broader reuse.

What happens next

  1. Open a public-safe request issue.
  2. RailsReady confirms whether the kit fits the stated pilot shape.
  3. Payment and delivery happen through a private channel once a payment link or invoice is available.

Safety boundary

RailsReady will not ask for private keys, seed phrases, wallet signatures, passwords, browser cookies, customer PII, bank statements, or confidential legal documents. If a request needs those materials, it is not a fit for this product.
